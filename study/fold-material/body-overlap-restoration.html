What did the repair change?

The target keeps the original slightly negative gap; it does not separate the triangles to zero. A gap is the upper triangle's height minus the lower one's at an overlap corner. The derivative includes both moving triangles and the moving corner. It predicts the gap change for a small movement; the actual gap is measured again afterward.

The repair minimizes the sum of squared movements of free vertices for this one local equation. Held vertices do not move. It does not minimize material energy or preserve every other contact automatically. The tiny repair's size is not the stopping test for the still-unsettled material direction.

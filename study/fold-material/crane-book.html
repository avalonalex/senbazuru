References and limits

The Tuttle book example uses broad fills and selected crease lines. Binnette’s vector crane and its photographic counterpart by Andreas Bauer show how clean contours can preserve a recognizable fold. Tinygami’s photographs show a fuller pillow body. These are visual references; the drawing here is generated from our own mesh.

This remains a static shape sketch with material distortion and intersections, not a simulated inflation. All three presentations use the same visible surface, including its defects. Pink coverage warnings, if present, are kept in every view. Omitted crease marks are an optional drawing choice, not a change to the crease pattern.

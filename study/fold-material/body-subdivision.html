Full proposals and shortened corrections

The solver proposes a movement, then may halve it repeatedly until cost decreases. Convergence requires a successful linear solve and a full proposal no larger than 0.0000001 sheet units, together with geometric tolerances. A tiny shortened movement alone is insufficient. A candidate is a line-search choice; at equilibrium the solver may return the preceding position instead. Returned checkpoints above are authoritative.

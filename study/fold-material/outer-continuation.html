Does more work settle the shape?

The original final stage had a 40-iteration limit. This continuation allows up to 40 more at length weight 1e10, using progressive contact exchange. Stopping still requires a converged local quadratic solve and full proposed movement ≤ 1e-7. Acceptance also requires relative edge error ≤ 1e-5, original crease error ≤ 1e-5 radians, exact holds and material identities, independent whole-sheet contact at 1e-7 and nonnegative exact directional gaps.

Both profiles use the same camera and scale. Teal is the lower panel; ochre is the upper panel. Maps sample the same projected locations: green near contact, ochre separated, red crossing and grey outside overlap. A contact-off shape stays diagnostic even if its solver settles.

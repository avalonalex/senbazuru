What was checked

All 120 panel pairs, all 28 material edge lengths, and 71 overlapping panel pairs across the two flat endpoints. Exact arithmetic checks the ideal motion between the pictures. Every displayed pose is rebuilt from crease angles and agrees with that path within 0.000000000001 of the sheet’s side length; shared vertices, achieved angles and contact are checked again.

This is zero-thickness paper following one known petal route. It does not yet check the square-base collapse, the second petal, or find a route for an arbitrary crease pattern. Complete paper in the 3D viewer retains coincident buried layers; Visible paper removes them for stable display.

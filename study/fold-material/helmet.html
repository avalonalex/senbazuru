Two layers, one turning flap

Fold a square diagonally in half, then bring each corner of the doubled triangle to its opposite tip. Turn both layers together each time. The resulting smaller square is the traditional helmet base.

Every turn starts where the previous one finished. The sheet stays joined, touching layers keep their order, and the contact check covers the whole turn, including its flat landing.

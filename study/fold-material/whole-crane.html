How to inspect the geometry

The first candidate keeps the saved body fixed. The pillow proposal instead changes the body and its four attachments together; the neck and tail follow without a separate weld. These are static guesses, not frames of a checked motion. The complete exports retain all buried triangles and original material coordinates; no separate parts were pasted onto the body. The preview clips triangles by their actual camera depth, including where they cross. Coincident planes use inherited order, with a recorded face-id tie when no order exists. Being able to draw a surface does not certify its geometry.

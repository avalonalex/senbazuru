What this comparison can establish

An accepted endpoint must preserve material lengths within 0.001%, exact holds, shared material and contact order, and meet the existing solver convergence checks. The 5° refined specimen uses the same controls with more triangles. Energy differences remain diagnostics. An invalid result remains visible here as a labelled wire diagram, never as an accepted paper pose.

Every view has the same drawing scale and fixed camera across runs. Side looks along the folded sheet, top looks down on it, and underside looks from below. Wire diagrams show hidden material and can look more tangled than the visible paper.

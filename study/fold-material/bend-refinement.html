What this does and does not establish

Agreement with the analytic integral tests this bending rule on two prescribed profiles. The curve parameters and material coefficient are fixed across every mesh. Each bending spring is still weighted using its material triangle sizes. This does not certify a coarse mesh for arbitrary paper shapes, establish force response or explain the old solved 8.99% gap by itself. Width-varying curvature, separated layers, physical calibration and continuously checked flexible motion remain separate work. No material law or acceptance threshold changes here.

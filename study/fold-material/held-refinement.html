Eligibility means both endpoints meet the 1e-8 stop and paper checks; it does not mean the refinement targets passed. Targets stay below 0.001 sheet lengths for whole-material displacement and below 5% for separate opening and energy changes. Near-zero opening (≤ 1e-7) or energy (≤ 1e-12) is compared absolutely. Displacement covers all material triangle overlaps, not just shared vertices.

A smaller final proposal does not bound all remaining motion or prove a global minimum. Width refinement, stiffness calibration and flexible folding routes remain separate work.

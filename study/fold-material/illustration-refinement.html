Distance between the exposed regions

For every point in one filled layer region, find the nearest point of that layer in the other mesh. The largest distance is bounded below and above, in both directions. This includes polygon interiors and tiny strips before SVG rounding or pixel thresholds. A bound spanning two pixels stays unresolved; a missing layer cannot pass. A large distance can come from a tiny distant fragment: it does not mean the sheet moved by that amount. These are floating-point geometric bounds, not exact-arithmetic certificates.

What this does and does not establish
Both endpoints must retain their original accepted status, reported numerical convergence and fresh material/contact checks. Contact-off remains diagnostic regardless of image similarity. Views with unresolved projected visibility receive no graded image. Energy changes stay in the material report; they do not gate this illustration experiment.
The two-pixel budget is an engineering choice for this scale and these four cameras. It is not a tolerance on paper penetration, permission to stretch the sheet, evidence of calibrated paper mechanics or a certificate covering a folding movement. Changing the output size or camera calls for another comparison.

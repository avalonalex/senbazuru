Why the direction matters

Turning the same wing the other way would depart through the layer it rests on. That control is refused because its departure contradicts the starting order. A crease gives a line to rotate around; the layers tell us which side the wing can move into.

The check covers the entire prescribed turn using floating-point interval bounds and numerical guards. The four illustrations are selected from that checked route. Separate measurements verify material lengths, shared vertices, achieved crease angles, contact and the fixed body.

This is one rigid movement of zero-thickness paper on the existing crane fixture. It does not check the preceding construction of the crane, lower its second wing, or inflate its body. Bending, sliding contact and finding coordinated movements remain separate work.

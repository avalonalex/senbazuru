“Source” retains the original 1e-7 stopping policy. Only the two finer meshes are continued: comparisons to 128 triangles still include the original, less tightly stopped coarser solve. Eligibility means both endpoints satisfy their stated stops and paper checks; it does not mean the refinement targets passed.

Targets stay below 0.001 sheet lengths for whole-material displacement and below 5% for separate opening/energy changes. Near-zero opening (≤ 1e-7) or energy (≤ 1e-12) is compared absolutely. Displacement covers all material triangle overlaps, not just shared vertices.

A smaller final proposal does not bound all remaining motion or prove a global minimum. Width refinement, stiffness calibration and flexible folding routes remain separate work.

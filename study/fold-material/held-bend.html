What remains open

Meeting the same grips and length checks does not determine a unique flexible shape. The fitted curves are a controlled family whose parameters change with the mesh. Their energies and those of the unchanged smooth reference must be compared separately before interpreting the saved 8.99% difference. Material-equilibrium optimization, width-varying bends, separation and a continuously checked flexible route remain separate work. No acceptance limit or material rule changes here.

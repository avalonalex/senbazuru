SENBAZURU · CHECKED FOLDING SEQUENCE

Four corners, one blintz base

Start with the coloured side facing you. Fold each corner to the centre; the four triangular flaps make a smaller square called a blintz base. Then reopen the first corner, leaving the other three folded.

Every turn starts where the previous one finished. The central square stays still, the sheet stays joined, and resting layers keep their order. The contact check covers each entire turn, including the flat landing and reopening.

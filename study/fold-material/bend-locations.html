What the saved endpoints show

On 4×1, the upper panel can turn at 15/32, halfway between the band end and the held outer edge. Both loaded rules use that new bend in the opposite direction to the preceding band bends. Matched holds barely bend there. The coarse mesh has no transverse hinge inside that outer strip, so a new row in the region table is partly a change in available bending locations, not a direct measurement of continuous energy density.

The matched control has a different pattern: its interior cost rises while costs on the two boundary lines fall. These observations identify an outer-strip refinement control worth testing; they do not yet establish why the optimized solutions differ.

Evidence and limits

Every saved endpoint is rechecked for unchanged material, shared vertices, holds, original crease angles and contact. Historical solver convergence is retained separately. Reported costs must agree with fresh measurements before the page is generated. Contact-off shapes remain diagnostic even when their energy is smaller.

This maps numerical endpoints. It does not establish a continuous folding route, a tighter-solve confirmation, calibrated paper properties or the refinement study's exit criteria. Both bending rules and all acceptance limits remain unchanged.

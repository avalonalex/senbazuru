Change where the priority goes

Every layout starts from the same 64-triangle mesh. Both concentrated layouts repeatedly bisect the interval with the greatest weighted length: density four in the chosen region, one outside. Equal scores choose the interval nearer the shared crease. Joins uses the previous windows within 1/32 sheet length of each curvature transition. Whole bend uses the complete interval from 1/8 through 1/8 plus the fixed bend length. These regions have different widths; neither width nor density is tuned against energy. The two width strips stay fixed.

What the comparison means

These are predeclared placement policies on two fixed, width-independent profiles. Better cost accuracy can accompany larger grip drift or edge shortening. This does not identify an optimal grid or an adaptive solver. All controls remain diagnostic, including any sampled mesh inside the numerical length cap. Material stiffness, triangle weighting and acceptance criteria are unchanged. The solved 8.99% gap, realistic force response, separated layers and a continuously checked flexible motion remain separate work.

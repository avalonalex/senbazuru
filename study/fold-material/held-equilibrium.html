Each comparison intersects the two triangulations on the original sheet and measures every overlap corner. That includes unmatched vertices and covers the entire piecewise-flat surface, even when it bends differently across the width. Targets remain displacement < 0.001 sheet lengths and separate opening/energy changes < 5%, using the first case as reference. Near-zero opening (≤ 1e-7) or energy (≤ 1e-12) gets an absolute difference, not a misleading percentage. A diagnostic comparison cannot establish accepted refinement.

This is one length-and-placement experiment, with fixed width and one solver policy. Width refinement, persistence of contact regions, a tighter solve, physical calibration and a valid route between states remain separate checks. The old solved 8.99% difference is not retroactively resolved.

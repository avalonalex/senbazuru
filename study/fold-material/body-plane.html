Triangle 70's plane is the flat surface through its three vertices, extended beyond its edges. Vertex 27 lies outside the original projected triangle. This guard is therefore a conservative local rule, not an additional physical overlap contact. Its sign follows the original triangle winding.

The derivative includes vertex 27 and vertices 81, 71 and 20 of triangle 70. Moving the triangle can turn its plane and change the distance even if vertex 27 stays still. The local prediction does not guarantee the finite trial: the actual plane and all triangle intersections are checked again.

What one repair is allowed to do

Vertex 27 is near the plane through triangle 70. A repair takes the smallest sum of squared vertex movements predicted to recover this correction's starting distance to that moving plane. It moves only free coordinates. The target can be slightly negative: this preserves the existing floor, rather than demanding exact separation.

Each material direction has twelve overlap guards for triangles 22–63, 14–55 and 46–70, plus the plane guard. A repair must preserve those guards and the overlap derivatives refreshed at the refused trial. Every candidate is rechecked against all triangle pairs and unchanged material lengths, holds and costs. A changed overlap configuration remains a refusal.

Contact tolerance remains 1e-7 sheet lengths, relative length error 1e-5 and linear guard residual 1e-12. The search has the same 31 fractions and stops at its first usable candidate; every visited raw trial and attempted repair is saved. Fractions after selection receive no repairs. Intersections inside the existing tolerance may remain visible in magnified views.

This is a body-patch experiment. It does not provide a continuously checked flexible motion, the omitted crane's loads or whole-crane inflation.

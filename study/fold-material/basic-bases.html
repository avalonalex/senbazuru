Six more ways to gather a square

Helmet, organ, frog, boat, pig and diamond bases. Each begins with one unit square and ends as a connected sheet with checked crease angles and layer order. Switch sides to reveal the flaps hidden on the back.

Closed endpoints. Figures are fitted independently; their displayed sizes do not compare material scale.

Mountain creases use dash-dot-dot lines, valleys use dashes, and flat guides use thin grey lines. These are verified flat endpoints of zero-thickness paper. They do not yet prescribe the folding motion or certify collisions between states. Spreading layers is a drawing aid; the FOLD downloads retain the shared vertices of the exact closed sheet.

Which part of the crossing test changes?

Imagine a triangle's flat plane extending beyond its outline. The other triangle straddles that plane when it has vertices beyond 1e-7 on both sides. A crossing is flagged only if both triangles straddle and their finite intersection segment is longer than 1e-7. Here the first two conditions are already true; the segment length is the condition that changes.

Largest cost is not always worst relative error

Length cost uses the absolute error: ½ × 1e10 × (current length − original length)². Relative error divides that difference by original length. A short edge can have a large relative error while a much longer edge has a larger absolute error and cost. Positive signed error means extension; negative means compression.
